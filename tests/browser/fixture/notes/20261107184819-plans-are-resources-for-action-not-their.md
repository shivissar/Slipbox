title: Plans are resources for action, not their determinants
type: zettel
---

Suchman's central move in *Plans and Situated Actions* is to reverse the cognitivist reading of plans. Plans do not determine action; actors draw on them as resources, before and after the fact, in activity that is always situated @suchmanPlansSituatedActions1987.

This sharpens [[20261107184818]]. If a waypoint mission is a plan in Suchman's sense, the pilot's post-flight account is less a report of execution than a repair of the gap between the plan and what the aircraft did.
