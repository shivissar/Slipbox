A note on terminology

The PolyForm Noncommercial license is source-available rather than "open source" in the strict technical sense. The Open Source Definition maintained by the Open Source Initiative prohibits any restriction on field of endeavor, including restrictions on commercial use, so licenses like this one are not OSI-approved open source. They are better described as "source-available" or "freely available for non-commercial use." The distinction matters mostly for people who are careful about licensing terminology; for most users, the practical difference is whether they work at a for-profit company that wants to ship a product using Zetteling (not allowed) or use it for their own research (allowed, regardless of employer).

The PolyForm project is a working group of experienced open-source lawyers who drafted this license specifically to fill the gap between permissive open-source licenses and commercial terms. It has been adopted by a growing number of academic and small-author projects for exactly the reasons Zetteling uses it. The canonical text and FAQ live at polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0.
