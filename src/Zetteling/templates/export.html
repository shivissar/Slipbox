Notes on PDF export

Pandoc requires a LaTeX engine (XeLaTeX, pdfLaTeX, etc.) to produce PDFs directly. For PDF, the easiest workflow is to export to HTML or Word first, then print-to-PDF from your browser or word processor. Or install MacTeX (brew install --cask mactex-no-gui) and pandoc will gain a pdf format option.
