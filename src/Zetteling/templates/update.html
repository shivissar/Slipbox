Installing it

  1. Download the new version (the button above). Your browser saves Zetteling-{{ L.version }}.dmg, usually in Downloads.
  2. Quit Zetteling (⌘Q). Anything you were typing has already been saved.
  3. Open the downloaded file and drag Zetteling onto Applications. When the Mac asks, choose Replace.
  4. So that exporting keeps working, run this line in Terminal again, as when you first installed:
    xattr -cr /Applications/Zetteling.app
  5. Open Zetteling. If macOS says it can’t check the app, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

Your notes, settings, note history and locked-notes key are not inside the app, so replacing it leaves them as they are. If the new version needs anything else from you, its notes above say so.
