Installing it

  1. Download the new version (the button above). Your browser saves Slipbox-{{ L.version }}.dmg, usually in Downloads.
  2. Quit Slipbox (⌘Q). It saves what you were typing first.
  3. Open the downloaded file and drag Slipbox onto Applications. When the Mac asks, choose Replace.
  4. Open Slipbox. If macOS asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet, click Open.

Your notes, settings, note history and locked-notes key are not inside the app, so replacing it leaves them as they are. If the new version needs anything else from you, its notes above say so.
